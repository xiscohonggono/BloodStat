## Goal

The FHIR Box dates in the pitch deck should be correct, worded carefully, and attributed to a source.

## Plan

- Compare every FHIR Box date in the deck with what MOHW has said.
- Current best source: an IBMI summary dated 7 Sep 2026 of a UDN report ([money.udn.com/money/story/7307/9675552](https://money.udn.com/money/story/7307/9675552)). It says:
  - Chien-Chang Lee, Director-General of MOHW's Department of Information Management, said FHIR Box is "scheduled to go live by the end of 2026" across all medical centres;
  - this followed pilots at three medical centres in 2025;
  - about 80% of healthcare facilities are planned within 3–5 years.
- The UDN report is a secondary source. Look for an MOHW press release or other primary source.
- The pitch falls at the deadline. Say "scheduled to go live by end of 2026", not "live", unless the launch is confirmed. Check the news the week before the pitch.
